Keep one ticket per supported cause, not one ticket per subject line. The review of #4471 shows why the distinction matters.

The rule

Decision 0002 replaces the subject-only rule in decision 0001. Keep that accepted record unchanged.

  1. Deduplicate exported rows by report ID.
  2. Read the body and resolve the tenant’s locale, form version and report channel.
  3. Compare supported causes with causeOf and sameCause, not subject text.
  4. Merge only known, matching causes. Keep unknown or conflicting cases for investigation.
  5. Keep each report’s ID and the count with its engineering ticket.
Different subjects can describe the same cause. Matching subjects can describe different causes. Browser locale and intake tags are not substitutes for the tenant directory.
