0002 · Match duplicates by cause, not subject

owner harness for Hexrays accepted reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

Context

The rendered review of #4471 shows distinct parser and form-hint causes merged into one ticket. Subject text cannot distinguish them. The interactive examples also show one cause across different subjects. Decision 0001 is stale under the kb review rule.

Decision

This record replaces the subject-only rule in decision 0001 for this revised kb. Keep the accepted old record unchanged.

Read the report, resolve its tenant and channel, and compare supported causes. In this export, use tenant locale and form version with causeOf and sameCause. Merge only when both causes are known and equal. A subject is a search hint, not evidence of the same problem. Unknown tenants, conflicting symptoms and timing anomalies need review.

Consequences

  • Engineering gets one ticket per supported cause, with unique report IDs and a count.
  • Different subject lines can merge; matching subject lines can stay apart.
  • Unknown and conflicting reports take more investigation, but do not disappear into a known ticket.
  • A change in locale, form or product needs new evidence and may require a new classifier.
  • Rebuild from each new export. A merged or queued ticket does not establish a fix.
